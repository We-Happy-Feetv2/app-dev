# Bullet Train (2022)

*A fast, funny, and very bloody ride from Tokyo to Kyoto.*

---

## A. Introduction

### Title

**Bullet Train**

### Overview

*Bullet Train* is a stylish action comedy about a group of assassins who all end up on the same high-speed train in Japan. Brad Pitt leads the cast as an unlucky hitman who only wants a quiet, simple job, but nothing on this trip goes according to plan. The film mixes over-the-top fight scenes, sharp humor, and a tangled plot where every passenger turns out to be connected. It is directed by David Leitch and is based on the Japanese novel *Maria Beetle* by Kotaro Isaka.

### Quick Details

- **Genre:** Action, Comedy, Thriller
- **Release Year:** 2022
- **Setting:** A bullet train traveling from Tokyo to Kyoto, Japan

---

## B. Synopsis

### The Setup

Ladybug is a hitman with famously terrible luck who is trying to take a calmer, less violent approach to his work. His handler sends him onto a bullet train for what should be an easy job: grab a briefcase and get off at the next stop. Unfortunately for him, the train is also carrying several other assassins who are after the same case or have their own deadly business onboard.

### The Ride

As the train speeds toward Kyoto, Ladybug keeps running into the other killers, including two bickering partners named Tangerine and Lemon, who are escorting a kidnapped young man and a case full of ransom money. Each time Ladybug tries to leave at a station, bad luck or a new enemy drags him back on board. Slowly, the passengers realize that their jobs are tied together and that someone powerful is pulling the strings behind the scenes.

### The Ending

Without spoiling everything, the story builds to a chaotic final showdown as the train races toward its last stop. Old grudges, family revenge, and a feared crime boss all collide in the last act. Most of the assassins do not survive the trip, but Ladybug's strange luck finally pays off in a way that fits the movie's tone perfectly.

## C. Characters

- **Ladybug (Brad Pitt):** An unlucky assassin who has been working on staying calm and avoiding violence, though trouble always finds him anyway.
- **Tangerine (Aaron Taylor-Johnson):** A sharp, confident, and dangerous assassin who is partnered with Lemon and acts like his brother.
- **Lemon (Brian Tyree Henry):** Tangerine's partner, a talkative and funny killer who is obsessed with the show *Thomas the Tank Engine* and uses it to understand people.
- **The Prince (Joey King):** A young woman who pretends to be an innocent schoolgirl while secretly manipulating everyone around her.
- **Kimura (Andrew Koji):** A father out for revenge after his young son was seriously hurt, who is forced to team up with others on the train.
- **The Elder (Hiroyuki Sanada):** Kimura's wise and skilled father, who is calm, honorable, and deadly with a sword.
- **White Death (Michael Shannon):** A feared crime boss whose son and money are at the center of the entire conflict.

## D. Personal Review

### Why I Chose This Movie

I chose *Bullet Train* because I enjoy action comedies and I was curious to see Brad Pitt in a funnier, more relaxed role. The idea of a whole movie happening on one train also sounded unique, and I wanted to see how the story would keep things exciting in such a small space.

### What I Liked

1. **Brad Pitt's performance.** He plays Ladybug with great comedic timing, and his calm, therapy-speak attitude makes the violence even funnier.
2. **The colorful style and action.** The fight scenes are creative, fast, and full of humor, and the bright neon look of Japan makes every scene fun to watch.
3. **The connected storyline.** Small details that seem random at the start end up mattering later, which makes the plot satisfying to follow.
4. **The strong supporting cast.** Characters like Lemon and Tangerine steal scenes with their friendship and banter.

### My Rating

**8 / 10**

### Recommendation

> I recommend *Bullet Train* to anyone who likes fast, funny action movies and does not mind some graphic violence. It is not a deep story, but it is a very entertaining ride with great characters and a lot of surprises. *Watch it with friends* and enjoy the chaos.
